Edit what musical and visual elements are mapped to natural data:

Drag a line from a natural data element to a musical or visual element to map them together. Click the ring below the connected element to open the slider. The slider controls how strongly that data element affects the musical or visual element. 0% has no effect, 100% is a normal effect, and over 100% is an exaggerated effect. Click the green check to save the slider and close it out, and click the red ‘x’ to disconnect completely.
